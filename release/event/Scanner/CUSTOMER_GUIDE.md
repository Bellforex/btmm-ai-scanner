# RC5 Scanner — Getting Access

The scanner is published on TradingView as an **invite-only** script. Access is
granted to your TradingView account individually.

## 1. Send your TradingView username

Send the **exact username** of your TradingView account — not your email
address, and not your display name.

To find it: click your avatar (top right on TradingView). The username is shown
in the menu and in your profile URL, `tradingview.com/u/YOUR_USERNAME/`.

Please double-check the spelling. Access is granted to exactly the name you
give, so a typo grants someone else and leaves you waiting.

## 2. Access is added manually

Your username is added to the script's access list with an expiry matching your
plan. You will be told when it is active.

## 3. Open the scanner

1. open any chart on TradingView
2. click **Indicators**
3. choose **Invite-only scripts**
4. click **RC5 Scanner** to add it to your chart

If it is not listed, refresh the page — access changes can take a moment to
appear.

## 4. Two scripts, designed to run together

**CORE** does the analysis and draws the points of interest. **VIEW** draws the
market-structure overlay. They are separate because of TradingView's size
limits, and they are intended to be on the chart at the same time.

Note that TradingView's Basic plan limits how many indicators a chart can hold.

## 5. Expiry and renewal

Access carries an expiry date. When it lapses the scanner simply stops being
available; nothing is deleted from your account. Message us to renew and your
access is extended.

## 6. Access is personal

Access is tied to your TradingView account and cannot be shared, sold or
transferred. There is no link or code that grants access to someone else — each
user must be added individually.

## 7. What it is, and what it is not

The scanner marks **market structure and points of interest**: confirmed
swings, breaks of structure, and the zones where structure was decided.

It does not predict price, it is not a signal service, it makes no performance
claim, and it is not financial advice. It shows you where the market made a
decision; what you do with that is your trading.
